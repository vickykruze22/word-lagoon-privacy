Word Lagoon — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Word Lagoon: Calm Word Puzzle ("the app") is a single-player word game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, coins, daily streak and settings) is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device through the app. If your device has Android's backup feature turned on, Google may include this data in your personal device backup under your own Google account. You can erase it at any time with "Reset progress" in the app's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app asks for vibration (for optional touch feedback, which you can switch off in settings) and the standard internet permission required by the technology the app is built with. The app works fully offline and does not send your data anywhere.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
